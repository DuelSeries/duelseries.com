PAC-MAN LAB

Three ways to make multiplayer Pac-Man work, playable with bots. Local only, nothing here is on the site.

1Royale Rounds

Up to 4 on one screen. One fewer power pellet than players. Powered you eat rivals, same state you bump. Knocked out, you play a ghost. Last one standing takes the round, first to 3.

1bRoyale Rounds, 1v1

The same game as a paid duel would run it: two players, mirror corners.

2Ghost Train Duel

You and a bot on the same maze from the same seed, side by side. Build a train of sleeping ghosts, then a pellet and eat the line. Clear half the maze to speed up. Highest score in 2:30 wins.

3Train Arena

Today's arena, calmer, with one new idea: your ghost train is a weapon. A rival who touches it is caught and their money is yours. Pretend $1 seats, hold Q to cash out.
Every page has its own speed choices at the top, so you can feel slow against fast. Add ?auto=1 to any page to watch a bot play your seat.
