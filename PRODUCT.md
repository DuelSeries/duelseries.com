# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Players who come to a browser game site to play quick arcade games (snake, agar, tank arena, paper territory, battle royale) against real people, for free or for a small stake. They play on a PC and on phones, often in short sessions, and many arrive knowing slither.io or agar.io already. Owen, the owner, is also a daily player and the only admin.

## Product Purpose

DuelSeries (duelseries.com) runs familiar multiplayer browser games where a player can enter a paid room for a small USDC stake and leave with their winnings, or play free. Success is players who come back, play together, and trust that money in and out is honest.

## Positioning

Familiar games played for real stakes, with a self-custody wallet each player controls (Privy) and payouts that can be checked on-chain, instead of a house-held balance.

## Operating Context

The lobby (public/v2.html) is the hub: Home (game rail), Wallet, Events (battle royale), Leaderboards, and PC-only Clips and Updates, plus Settings. Games run in an iframe beside the lobby. Phones get a bottom tab bar; PCs get a left sidebar.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Minimum age 21. Legal pages are drafts pending lawyer review.
- Copy never promises winnings; it says what a thing is, not what anyone can win.
- No referral payouts or anything that moves money outside the wallet flows.
- Wallet words: "Add funds" and "Withdraw"; "Cash out" only means leaving a game with winnings.
- Very early stage: few real players yet, so community channels (Discord, Whop, socials) are being set up now and may not have links yet.
- Whop is planned as a creator program (Owen, 2026-10-16): people join the DuelSeries team on Whop, post clips or ads for DuelSeries, and get paid per views through Whop's content rewards. Rates and terms are undecided; never state a rate until Owen sets one.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: DuelSeries. Contact: playduelseries@gmail.com.
- Game looks follow slither.io and agar.io closely (slither palette for snake colours).
- No emoji as artwork. No em dashes in copy.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: the games, the leaderboards and cashed-out totals from the server, the Updates posts (public/updates.json), approved player clips. Absent and never to be invented: player counts, testimonials, prize promises, follower counts.

## Product Principles

1. The game is the hero; chrome stays quiet so the artwork reads as lit.
2. Honest money: every figure shown is real and checkable.
3. Simple for everyone: plain words, big targets, nothing to learn.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Owen's PC reports prefers-reduced-motion, so motion-gated effects must still look finished without motion. Phone touch targets 44px or more.
