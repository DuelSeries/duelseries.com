Who we are

DuelSeries (duelseries.com, "we", "us") runs this site. You can reach us at playduelseries@gmail.com.

Who can play

You must be at least 21 years old, and playing skill games for money must be legal where you live. It is up to you to check the law that applies to you. Owen to fill places where paid play is not offered

Your account and wallet

You sign in with Privy, using a Google account or an email address. Signing in creates a Solana wallet that only you control. Your wallet address is your account here. We never hold your money and never have your keys.

Keep your sign-in secure. Anything sent from your wallet is final: transfers on the Solana network cannot be reversed by you, by us or by anyone else.

Money

  • Prices and amounts are shown in US dollars. Entry fees and payouts are paid in SOL on the Solana network, at the SOL price when you join or are paid.
  • Paid games have fixed buy-ins of $0.50 or $1.00. When you join, your buy-in goes from your wallet to the game's escrow wallet.
  • When you cash out, the escrow sends 90% of your worth in that game to your wallet. The other 10% is the house fee.
  • If a paid game you bought into cannot take you, your buy-in is refunded to the wallet it came from.
  • Funds added in any token other than SOL or USDC, funds added on any network other than Solana, and withdrawals to a wrong address cannot be recovered.

Free play and events

Free games cost nothing and pay nothing. The Battle Royale, every 6 hours, is free to enter; the last player left wins $20, paid to their wallet in SOL.

Rooms, free or paid, may include computer players run by us. In a paid room one may carry the room's stake, funded by us, which whoever knocks it out wins. They never cash out and are never paid: money one picks up drops back into play when it is knocked out, and if it leaves the room any other way, that money goes back to the players it came from, never to us. In a room they played in, money nobody picks up goes back to the player who dropped it.

Fair play

Win with your own skill. Bots, scripts, modified game clients, playing several accounts together and teaming up against other players are not allowed. We watch for them. Owen to fill what happens to results and payouts when cheating is found
