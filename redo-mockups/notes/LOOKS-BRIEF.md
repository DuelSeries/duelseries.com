# DuelSeries site redo: Stage 2, three full-site looks (mockups only)

DuelSeries (duelseries.com) is a real-money browser skill-game site: 16 game cards (13 playable), free rooms and paid rooms ($0.50 / $1 entry), money is SOL in a self-custody Privy wallet but every figure shows in dollars. A free Battle Royale (Slither.io engine) runs every 6 hours with $20 to 1st. Traffic is tiny, so the site must look polished and trustworthy at first sight. Over months the tabs were built one at a time and don't match. Owen (the owner) wants the whole site redone so every tab, sheet, toast, game HUD and end screen fits one system and looks great on PC (1440x900) and on his phone (Samsung S24, 384x832). He picks one of three looks; the pick becomes the shared style kit that Stage 3 rebuilds every screen on.

**Mockups only.** Never touch `slither-clone/` or `wt-site-redo/`. Work only in `C:/Users/owend/Documents/Coding Projects/Claude Code Test/site-redo/looks/<your letter>/`.

## What each look delivers (files in `looks/<a|b|c>/`)
1. `kit.css`: the look's whole style kit as CSS custom properties plus components: type scale, colours, radii, spacing, buttons (primary, secondary, quiet, danger, disabled, loading), game card, panel, list row, tabs/segmented control, input, sheet/modal (PC centred, phone bottom sheet), toast (ok, err, info), chips (live, new, soon, money), the PC sidebar + header and the phone header + bottom bar. Every page below uses only this kit plus small page-specific CSS.
2. `home.html`: the lobby. Header with the balance chip ($24.60, signed in), PC sidebar (Home, Wallet, Events, Leaderboards, Clips, Updates; Shop marked Soon; Live games list; pinned Battle Royale, Refer a friend, Join us, Support, Settings) and phone bottom bar (Home, Wallet, Events, Leaderboards, Settings). Content: the Live wins strip (small art thumbs, name, green amount), the Battle Royale (next match, $20 to 1st, free entry), all 16 games with real art (13 playable, Battleship, Stumble Guys, OMG Shooter marked Coming soon), player counts where the data has one, New tags, and a way to pick Free / $0.50 / $1 for a game (a sheet that opens from a card).
3. `wallet.html`: signed in, $24.60 / 0.164 SOL. Balance, **Add funds** and **Withdraw**, wallet address with Copy, Transactions, Earnings. The Add funds sheet must open (Pay with card, Receive from another wallet, From Phantom) and the Withdraw sheet must open to its first step (amount + address, a disabled "Send it" until filled). A signed-out state behind `?signedout` (one clear Sign in).
4. `events.html`: the Battle Royale in all four states via `DSKit.states(...)` (waiting, soon, live, over; see the data notes). Soon is the default: the join window is open, the clock is hot, Join is the loudest thing on the screen.
5. `end.html`: the shared game end screen as seen over a dimmed game frame (use a card art crop as the blurred backdrop): `?r=win` "You won $0.90" with Play again, Back to lobby, Share; `?r=lose` "Knocked out" with the same buttons; a paid result shows the money, a free one doesn't.
6. `kit.html` (PC only): a one-page sheet of the kit: colours, type scale, every button state, chips, a card, a list row, an input, a toast of each kind, a sheet. Owen sees the system at a glance; Stage 3 builds from it.

Every page: `<link rel="stylesheet" href="kit.css">` and `<script src="../assets/data.js"></script>` (data only, draws no chrome: build your own). Game art: `../assets/img/<id>-card.webp` (360x540 portrait, names painted in; crop with object-fit and object-position, never stretch). Fonts from Google Fonts with system fallbacks. Responsive in one file: must look finished at **1440x900** (and sane at 1920x1080) AND at **384x832** with a real phone layout, not a squeezed PC. Fully clickable: hover, press and focus states, sheets open and close (Escape, backdrop, close button), segmented controls switch, toasts show.

## Data (`window.DSKit`, read the header of `../assets/data.js`)
- `DSKit.data.games`: the live lineup and order (id, name, img, players, kind arena|duel|solo|soon, isNew, blurb).
- `br(state)` for state waiting|soon|live|over: prize 20 ("$20 to 1st"), free entry, every 6 h, `nextInSec`, `endsInSec`, `alive`, `players`, `podium`, `lastWinner`, `schedule`, `history`. Waiting = next match countdown + last podium; soon (under 10 min) = join window open, clock hot, Join loudest; live = "Live now", time until the circle closes, Join disabled as "Doors shut", Watch live leads; over = winner + prize, next match countdown, Join "Opens soon".
- `me` (balanceUsd 24.60, sol, solUsd, address), `txs` (kinds entry | payout | add | withdraw), `myCashouts`, `board`, `profile(name)`, `online`.
- Helpers: `DSKit.fmt.usd/sol/short/date/time/ago/hms/dur`, `DSKit.icon(name,size)` (home wallet events leaderboards clips updates eye play copy plus add withdraw search clock users bell card qr swap medal flame discord gift chev x check ext ...), `DSKit.countUp(el, value)`, `DSKit.states(list, render)`, `DSKit.every(fn)`, `DSKit.esc`. `DSKit.toast` is today's style: build your own toast in kit.css instead.
- Live wins: real recent wins look like `{name:'MrPeanutK', game:'snake', amount:1.77}`; use `DSKit.data` names and amounts, never invent a feed that claims to be live real money beyond the strip the site already has.

## Hard rules (a look that breaks one is thrown out)
1. Wallet words: **"Add funds"** and **"Withdraw"**. Never "Deposit" and never "Cash out" in the wallet. "Cash out" is only the in-game action.
2. No new blue or purple UI colour anywhere (game art is exempt). No em dashes anywhere, in copy or comments shown on screen. No emoji as UI. Sentence case. No uppercase letter-spaced eyebrow labels on top of headings.
3. Dollar count-up only on: the Events prize, leaderboard money, wallet balances. Every other figure is static.
4. Never gate a visible effect behind `prefers-reduced-motion`: Owen's PC reports reduce, so the look must be complete and alive without motion. Motion answers a press or marks the one live thing; no fade-up on every section.
5. Honest copy: plain words, no money promises ("guaranteed", "win big", "instant payout"), no invented player counts beyond the mockup data, no testimonials.
6. Phone: touch targets 44px or more, nothing under the bottom bar, no sideways scroll, text never cut.
7. Avoid the generic kit: identical rounded cards with the same grey shadow everywhere, gradient washes as decoration, a monospace face for every small label, "→" on every button, tracked caps eyebrows.
