Ideas for you

Taste calls, not bugs, so nothing here was changed. Most worth your time first.

  1. Legal pages Terms, Privacy and Responsible play still say Draft and show bracketed blanks like [retention period] and [limits of liability]; worth finishing with a lawyer before advertising

  2. Leaderboards tab Leaderboard rows say 16 cash-outs even for duels like Knockout and Battleship, where a player wins a table; 16 wins may read better there

  3. Wallet tab, signed out Signed out, the Wallet tab is just one Sign in button on an empty screen; one line on what the wallet is for would help new players

  4. Events tab, 360 wide On a small phone the sticky Join bar half covers the How it works line on the first view of Events

  5. Battleship fleet placing, phone Battleship ship tray: the names under each ship (CARR, BATT, CRUI, SUBM, DEST) are 7 px abbreviations nobody can read on a phone; full names or no names

  6. Air Hockey in game, phone Air Hockey on phones: the chat button sits on the top left corner of the rink; it could go next to the pause button in the strip instead

  7. 8 Ball Pool in game, phone upright 8 Ball Pool held upright: the whole table and both name panels are turned sideways, so names read top to bottom and the cue runs off the top; maybe ask players to turn the phone like Head Soccer does

  8. Free end card, Crossy Road vs Swim Swim's free end card shows a red loss figure (195 metres) while every other free duel shows Free; pick one look for free games

  9. Rooftop Snipers HUD, phone sideways The ping and FPS box sits on the corner of Rooftop's Shoot button (and Swim's Gas, Bowmasters' Fire) on a sideways phone; it is the same spot in every game by your choice, so maybe lift it on these pages only

  10. Slither.io free HUD (phone) Free slither.io (and agar.io) say CASH OUT on the hold button while free Paper.io says LEAVE; worth one word for free tables everywhere

  11. Slither.io old engine death and cash-out cards The old slither.io page (paid snake on the old engine) still has its own end cards: Watch, Play again, Lobby, 'House cut' and 'You received', where every other game says Lobby, Spectate, Play again, 'House 10%' and 'You receive'; move it to the shared card or retire it

  12. Share picture, Pac-Man The share picture and the invite link preview call Pac-Man Chomp, while the lobby card says Pac-Man; pick one name (Chomp keeps clear of the trademark)

  13. PC sidebar On a 1366x768 laptop the sidebar's Live games list stops mid-row behind the Battle Royale card, and Rooftop Snipers is cut to Rooftop Sn... beside its New pill

  14. Wallet tab, signed in The wallet's Add funds and Withdrawals heading has a capital W while every other heading is sentence case

  15. Clips tab (PC) The Clips tab is wider than the header on a 1366 laptop and narrower on 1920, so its edges never line up with the logo and Sign in

  16. Customize, phone Customize says Scroll to change skin on phones, where you swipe

  17. Clips tab, Waiting for you (owner) In your Clips queue the Take down and Watch on X row moves up or down with the caption length, so buttons in one row of cards do not line up (owner only)

  18. Battleship start of a bot match The Playing a bot pill covers the top of the board for its 5 seconds in Battleship (and in Knockout it sits over the strip); a spot under the board would hide nothing

  19. Battleship Coming soon page The Battleship Coming soon page uses the phone's plain font, its text nearly touches the screen edges and its button is 37 px tall; it could use the game pages' card look

  20. Free waiting card first paint The free waiting card first says Looking for an opponent, Nothing staked, then a second later Looking for a player 0:08, If nobody joins you'll play a bot; one wording from the first frame would read calmer (all duels)

  21. Bowmasters end card Bowmasters is locked but still has its own old end card and wait words; move it to the shared end card before it unlocks

  22. Rooftop Snipers rotate card Rooftop Snipers held upright: the chat button floats on the red Turn your phone sideways card

  23. Agar.io menu Before agar.io comes back: its menu's Spectate button is 32 px tall (29 on a small phone) and the card has an empty band under it

  24. Paper.io HUD (phone) On phones the ping and FPS readout clips the bottom of Paper.io's minimap by a few pixels (agar.io's Eject button too); the minimaps could sit a little higher

  25. Slither.io /sl direct visit /sl opened on its own (not from the lobby) is only a name box and Play, with no game name and no way back to the lobby
