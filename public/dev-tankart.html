Tank art

Drawn in code, no image files. Every size and colour was read off the reference's own sprite atlas rather than estimated: a tile is 54, a tank body 41×42 with 10-thick tracks and a 34×22 hull, the turret sprite 54×39 pivoting at 23.5, 19.5, crates and barrels 35. Grass is #3a8145, stone #a29184, the player hull #650a0a with #f5821f fittings.

The three tanks

Left is ours. The middle and right are the reference's own two palettes, matched exactly — its player is dark red with orange fittings, its enemies amber with brown tracks. A free-for-all needs a third that reads as you at a glance, which its single-player game never had to solve.
