Who can play

Free games are for anyone 13 or older and need no account. To make an account or play for money you must be at least 21 years old, and playing skill games for money must be legal where you live. It is up to you to check the law that applies to you. The site does not check your age or where you are, so by playing for money you tell us both are true.

We do not block any country or region, so checking the law where you live is up to you.

One person, one account. Do not play paid games for someone else, or let someone else play on your account.

Your account and wallet

Sign-in is run by Privy, a separate company. You can sign in with a Google account, an email address or a Solana wallet app (Phantom, Solflare or Backpack). If you use Google or email, signing in creates a Solana wallet for you that only you control. If you use a wallet app, that wallet is the one you play with. Either way, your wallet address is your account here.

We never have your keys and cannot move money out of your wallet. The only time your money is out of your wallet is while it is in a paid game: then it sits in our escrow wallet, which holds the money in play and pays out at the end (see Paid games).

Your money is held as SOL, the Solana network's own coin. The site shows it in US dollars, using a live SOL price checked against several price sources. The price of SOL moves, so your balance in dollars can go up or down even when you are not playing. If no reliable price is available, paid games and payouts wait until one is.

Keep your sign-in secure. Anything sent from your wallet is final: transfers on the Solana network cannot be reversed by you, by us or by anyone else.

Adding and withdrawing

  • Add funds opens Privy's funding sheet. Paying by card is done by MoonPay, a separate company, on its own screen, under its own terms, checks and fees. You can also send SOL on the Solana network to your wallet address. Send only SOL on Solana: anything else sent to that address is lost.
  • Convert turns USDC left in your wallet from before the site moved to SOL into SOL, through Jupiter, a separate service. The price can move a little before it lands. If it would pay less than the "at least" amount shown, nothing is converted.
  • Withdraw sends money from your wallet to any Solana address you choose. You pay the Solana network fee from your wallet, and 0.001 SOL always stays in your wallet to cover fees. Check the address: a withdrawal to a wrong address cannot be reversed or recovered.
  • We charge no fee to add, convert or withdraw. MoonPay, Jupiter and the Solana network may charge their own.

Paid games

  • Prices and amounts are shown in US dollars. Entry fees and payouts are paid in SOL on the Solana network, at the SOL price when you join or are paid.
  • Paid games have fixed buy-ins of $0.50 or $1.00. When you press Play on a paid game, your buy-in goes from your wallet to our escrow wallet straight away. You pay the small network fee on that transfer; we pay it on payouts.
  • Arena games (the snake, cell, Paper and maze games): you start worth your buy-in, and your worth changes as you win or lose money in the game. When you cash out, the escrow sends 90% of your worth in that game to your wallet. The other 10% is the house fee. If you are knocked out first, you lose what you held, buy-in included.
  • Duels (one against one): both players put in the same buy-in. The winner gets both buy-ins less a 10% house fee ($1.80 from a $1 duel), and the loser loses their buy-in. A draw gives both buy-ins back. Cancel while nobody has taken your table and your buy-in comes back in full.
  • Money dropped in an arena that nobody picks up goes to the house after a while: in the Paper and maze games after an hour, and in the snake game once the room has been empty for five minutes. Rooms that had computer players in them work differently (see Computer players).
  • The site always asks for the exact buy-in. If more than that arrives, you get the buy-in it covers and the extra is not returned.

If something goes wrong

  • You disconnect in an arena game. In the snake and Paper games, your player keeps going for a few seconds and is then knocked out, and what you held drops into play for others to pick up. In the cell and maze games, after a few seconds your player stops and stays still for three minutes, where it can still be eaten or caught. Then whatever is left is cashed out to your wallet as usual (90% to you, 10% house fee).
  • You disconnect in a duel. Some games hold your seat for five seconds. After that, leaving a match counts as a loss and your opponent is paid. If both players drop at the same time, there is no winner and both buy-ins are refunded.
  • Our server restarts or crashes during a paid game. What you held in that game goes back to your wallet in full, with no house fee. A pool table whose result was already decided pays its winner instead. Money dropped in an arena that nobody had picked up yet goes to the house, except in rooms that had computer players in them. The one exception is the snake game's older engine, which we only use while the current one is switched off: a restart there is not refunded.
  • A game cannot take you after your buy-in has landed (it is full, closed or the join fails), or you do not use a buy-in within five minutes: it is refunded automatically to the wallet it came from, with no house fee. A buy-in that confirms too late to be used is refunded the same way.
  • A payout cannot be sent right away. It is recorded and sent again automatically until it goes through. If one still has not arrived, write to us.
  • There are no other refunds. Money lost in a game you played is not returned.

Computer players

Some players on DuelSeries are computer players (bots) run by us. Here is where you will meet them and what they can do with money.

  • Free arenas (snake, cell, Paper and maze games) have computer players in them. They have ordinary player names, are not marked as bots, and the player counts in the lobby include them.
  • Free duels: if no person takes your table within about ten seconds, you play a computer player.
  • Paid arenas (snake, cell, Paper and maze games) can also have computer players in them, added by us. They look like any other player: ordinary names, not marked as bots, and counted in the lobby. Each one starts carrying the room's buy-in, paid from our own money. They play like anyone else: they can knock you out and pick up the money you drop, and you can knock them out and win what they carry. They never cash out and are never paid. When one is knocked out, everything it held drops into play. If one leaves any other way, the money it picked up goes back to the players it came from, and its own buy-in comes back to us. In a room they played in, money nobody picks up goes back to the player who dropped it.
  • Paid duels ($0.50 or $1) can be against a computer player run by us. When one is, the table shows "Bot opponent waiting" before you pay, and the match names it a bot. You only meet one if you pressed Play while that showed. It puts in the same buy-in from our own money and plays by the same game rules. If you win, you are paid exactly as against a person. If it wins, we keep your buy-in. How well a computer player plays depends on the game, and some are hard to beat.
  • The Battle Royale fills up with computer players. A computer player that wins is never paid a prize.

Free play and events

Free rooms cost nothing and pay nothing, and need no sign-up. The Battle Royale below is the one free game with a prize.

The Battle Royale runs every six hours (2 AM, 8 AM, 2 PM and 8 PM Eastern time) and is free to enter. The last person left wins $20, paid to their wallet in SOL. The prize is paid automatically when at least two different people played in the match, the winner and at least one other player have played a paid game before, and the winner's wallet and network have not won an automatic prize in the last seven days. Otherwise the prize is held for us to check by hand. A match with only one person in it, or one cut short by a server restart, pays no prize.

The Swim race event is free and has no prize.

Skins

Skins change how you look in a game and nothing else. They cost $1 to $5, paid in SOL from your wallet at the live price. Once bought, a skin is yours on every device. Skins are final, so there are no refunds.

Chat and clips

By sharing a clip you let us show it on DuelSeries and on its own share page. Only share clips you have the right to share. You can delete your own clips at any time.
