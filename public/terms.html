Your account and wallet

You sign in with Privy, using a Google account or an email address. Signing in creates a Solana wallet that only you control. Your wallet address is your account here. We never hold your money and never have your keys.

Keep your sign-in secure. Anything sent from your wallet is final: transfers on the Solana network cannot be reversed by you, by us or by anyone else.

Money

  • Balances are in USDC on the Solana network.
  • Paid games have fixed buy-ins of $0.50 or $1.00. When you join, your buy-in goes from your wallet to the game's escrow wallet.
  • When you cash out, the escrow sends 90% of your worth in that game to your wallet. The other 10% is the house fee.
  • If a paid game you bought into cannot take you, your buy-in is refunded to the wallet it came from.
  • Deposits of any token other than USDC, deposits on any network other than Solana, and withdrawals to a wrong address cannot be recovered.
