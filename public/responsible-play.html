Responsible play

Draft, last updated November 5, 2026

Draft. This page has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not final. Highlighted parts are still to be confirmed.

You can lose money

Paid games on DuelSeries are skill games played for real money. Every paid game can end with you losing your buy-in, and in arena games everything you held. Skill helps, but nobody wins every time, and some of the players you meet are computer players run by us that can win your money (see the Terms). Your money is held as SOL, so its value in dollars also moves up and down on its own.

Only play for money you can afford to lose.

Who can play for money

Paid games are only for players 21 and over [Owen to confirm: 21 is the minimum age], where playing skill games for money is legal. The site does not check your age, so please do not play for money if you are under age, and do not let anyone under age use your account.

Keep it a game

  • Decide what you can spend before you play, and stop when it is gone.
  • Do not chase losses, and never play to pay bills or debts.
  • Do not play when you are upset, tired or have been drinking.
  • Take breaks. Every game can be played free.
  • Buy-ins on DuelSeries are $0.50 or $1.00 a game, but many small games still add up.

What you can do today

The site does not have spending limits, time limits or a self-exclusion button yet. These are the things you can do now:

  • Play free. Every game has free rooms that cost nothing and pay nothing, and need no sign-up.
  • Keep only what you mean to play with in your game wallet. Withdraw the rest to another wallet that the site cannot reach.
  • Do not add funds when you have used up what you set aside. Card payments only happen when you start one through Add funds.
  • Check your history. Your Wallet lists every transaction, buy-ins and payouts included, and your Profile shows your earnings, so you can see what you have really spent.
  • Sign out to step away.

Getting help

If playing for money has stopped being fun, talk to someone. It is free and confidential.

  • Ontario: ConnexOntario, 1-866-531-2600, any time of day.
  • United States: the National Council on Problem Gambling helpline, by phone, text or chat.
