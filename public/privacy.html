Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Who we are

DuelSeries (duelseries.com, "we", "us") runs this site and decides what happens to the information on this page. Reach us through the site's Contact page or the DuelSeries Discord with any question about it.

Free games are for anyone 13 or older and need no account. Accounts and paid games are only for players 21 and over.

What we keep

  • Your wallet address, which is your account.
  • The player name you choose.
  • Your game record: games played, time played, high scores, and every buy-in, cash-out, prize and refund, with its amount, game and time.
  • Money records: the Solana transactions of your buy-ins and payouts, the house fee taken from each cash-out with your name and wallet, payouts we still owe you, and skins you bought.
  • Fair play records: money that moves between accounts in paid games, and pairs of accounts we flag for a closer look.
  • Battle Royale prizes: for each prize, the winner's wallet, name and internet address, used to stop one person collecting prizes on several accounts.
  • Technical details such as your internet (IP) address and browser, which our servers see on every request. We use them for security and to limit abuse, for example to cap how many paid seats one network can take in a room. Apart from Battle Royale prizes, we do not store your IP address with your account.
  • Not your email address, your real name or your card details. Accounts made before sign-in moved to Privy may still hold the email address and picture that came with an old Google sign-in. Nothing collects them now.

Signing in

Privy runs sign-in and creates your wallet. If you sign in with Google or an email address, Privy handles those details under its own privacy policy. Our server asks Privy only which wallet is yours. Privy's sign-in tokens are kept in your browser while you are signed in, and they can include your email address; they are removed when you sign out.

Analytics

The lobby and the game pages use PostHog to show us how the site is used. These legal pages do not. PostHog records:

  • the pages you open, what you click, errors, and game events such as paid games started and finished, buy-ins, refunds, withdrawals and adding funds;
  • where you came from: the site that sent you and any campaign tags in the link;
  • recordings of your session on the site. Anything you type, the sign-in and funding windows, wallet addresses and balances are hidden in them;
  • your IP address and browser, which reach PostHog through our own domain.
  • Once you sign in, PostHog links all of this to your wallet address. Our server also sends PostHog the money events of paid games under your wallet address, never a key, signature, email or balance.
  • There is no way to turn analytics off on the site today.

Chat and clips

Chat in a game is sent live, with your player name, to the players in that game. We do not save chat messages.

Clips: if you share a link to a clip on X, YouTube or TikTok, we keep the link, your caption, your player name, your X handle if the link has one, the game and your wallet address. If you share a clip recorded in a game, the recording is the last 30 seconds of the game screen and its sound, plus your microphone only if you turned it on. Nothing is uploaded unless you press Share. Recordings are stored on Amazon Web Services. We also keep a scrambled form of the uploader's IP address to limit abuse. The clips wall shows your name, caption, game, date and video, never your wallet address.

What is public

  • Your player name, earnings, games played, time played, join date, win rate, and every cash-out with its amount and time appear on your profile, and your name and results appear on the leaderboards.
  • Anyone who has your wallet address can look up the same profile by it, including your buy-ins.
  • Your player name and chat are seen by the other players in your game.
  • Everything sent to or from your wallet, including buy-ins and payouts, is recorded on the Solana network, which anyone can read.

On your device

Your browser stores your player name, skins, sound and display choices, clip settings and the region you play in, so they are there next time. It also keeps:

  • Privy's sign-in cookies and tokens, while you are signed in;
  • if you sign in with a wallet app, a device key for 30 days that lets games recognise you without asking every time. It cannot move money;
  • where you first came from (the site that sent you and any campaign tags), for analytics, until you clear it;
  • PostHog's own cookie and storage;
  • while a game tab is open, your name, wallet address and entry details for that game.
  • We use no advertising cookies. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this.

Who else handles it

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. Only the services that run the site handle it, each for its own part:

  • Privy: sign-in and wallets.
  • MoonPay: card payments when you add funds. You give your payment details and any identity checks to MoonPay, not to us, under its own privacy policy.
  • Jupiter: Convert. It sees your wallet address.
  • Amazon Web Services: our servers, the database and clip storage. Our main game server and the database are in Canada; a second game server in Ireland is used only while it is switched on.
  • Helius and the public Solana network: our connection to Solana. They see wallet addresses and transactions we look up or send.
  • PostHog: analytics, in the United States.
  • ntfy: alerts we send to our own phone, which can include your player name, the game and the buy-in you joined, or a Battle Royale winner's name.
  • Google Fonts: the site's typeface, which your browser loads from Google.
  • YouTube, TikTok and X: when you play a clip, it plays from that site.

How long we keep it

  • Money records (buy-ins, payouts, refunds, fees and their transactions) are kept for 7 years, for tax.
  • Your account, name and game record are kept until you ask us to delete them, and then deleted within 30 days. Nothing deletes them automatically.
  • Chat is not kept.
  • Battle Royale prize records keep the last 100 prizes.
  • A clip recording that is never approved is deleted within 45 days. An approved clip stays until you or we remove it.
  • Our server logs include wallet addresses.

Your choices

  • Change your player name in Settings, and sign out at any time.
  • Delete a clip you shared, from the Clips tab.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove what is stored on your device.
  • To ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to have it deleted, reach us through the site's Contact page or the DuelSeries Discord and include your wallet address. There is no delete button on the site yet.
  • Records on the Solana network cannot be deleted by anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
